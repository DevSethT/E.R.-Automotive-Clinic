# ER Auto Website

A modern, responsive website built for **ER Auto**, an automotive business looking to establish a stronger online presence and give customers an easy way to learn about the company, view their work, and get in contact.

This project is being designed and developed from the ground up as a real-world client website.

## 🚗 Project Overview

The ER Auto website will provide customers with a simple and mobile-friendly way to:

* Learn about ER Auto
* View photos and videos of vehicles and previous work
* Browse available merchandise
* Find contact information
* Easily get in touch with the business
* Learn more about the company and the people behind it

The goal is to keep the website clean, easy to navigate, and focused on showing off ER Auto's work.

## 📄 Planned Pages

### Home

The main landing page introducing ER Auto and highlighting important information, recent work, and ways to contact the business.

### Gallery

A dedicated gallery for displaying photos and videos of vehicles, projects, and work completed by ER Auto.

### About

Information about ER Auto, the business, and the people behind it.

### Merchandise

A section for displaying ER Auto merchandise such as shirts and hats.

The initial focus will be showcasing merchandise available for purchase in-store, with the possibility of adding online purchasing later.

## 📞 Contact

Contact options will be placed throughout the website so visitors don't have to search for a dedicated contact page just to reach the business.

The site will include prominent **Contact Us** buttons in areas such as the navigation and homepage.

## 🎯 Project Goals

* Build a real website for an actual business
* Create a responsive design that works across desktop and mobile devices
* Make ER Auto's work easy to showcase through photos and videos
* Keep navigation simple and intuitive
* Provide clear ways for customers to contact the business
* Build the project using reusable React components
* Practice designing and developing a production-style website from scratch
